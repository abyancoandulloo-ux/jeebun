About Mauritius Airport Transfers

Your trusted partner for taxi and shuttle services in Mauritius

We are a locally owned and operated airport transfer service based in Mauritius. With years of experience, we specialise in providing reliable, comfortable, and professional private taxi transfers for travellers arriving at Sir Seewoosagur Ramgoolam International Airport.
