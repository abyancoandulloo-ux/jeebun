About Mauritius Transfers

Your trusted partner for seamless island travel

We are a locally owned and operated airport transfer service based in Mauritius. With years of experience, we specialise in providing reliable, comfortable, and professional private transfers for travellers arriving at Sir Seewoosagur Ramgoolam International Airport.

Our fleet includes modern standard cars, spacious family vehicles, and private minibuses — all maintained to the highest standards. We pride ourselves on punctuality, transparent pricing, and a warm Mauritian welcome that sets the tone for your stay.
